# Notice response

## Procedure

1. **Classify** the notice: information request, approval, denial, reduction,
   overpayment, hearing notice, or renewal reminder.
2. **Find the clock.** Every notice has one:
   - information request → the stated "respond by" date;
   - denial → 60 days for reconsideration (SSA), 10 days to keep benefits
     continuing, per the notice's own language;
   - overpayment → 30 days to request waiver or appeal;
   - Medicaid renewal → the stated month, usually before the 15th.
   Quote the notice's own sentence; do not substitute remembered rules.
3. **State** in one line: what they want, by when, what happens if ignored.
4. **Draft the response** — a letter or the enclosed form, referencing the case
   number and the notice date, listing enclosures. Plain, short, factual.
5. **Enclosures** — copies only, never originals, unless the notice demands an
   original. Certified mail or the portal receipt for anything with a deadline.
6. **Calendar** the follow-up: 14 days for a status check, 30 if the response was
   mailed.
7. **Inject** one line to Chris: program, deadline, what he must sign.

## Never

- Never let a deadline pass quietly because the response was "almost ready".
  A late-but-filed appeal beats a perfect unfiled one.
- Never advise abandoning an appeal without naming the consequence in dollars and
  in coverage.
