# granular-recovery
## Granular Recovery

1. Identify exact missing state pieces and the last known good granular step.
2. Do not attempt full regeneration from memory or scratch.
3. Rebuild from the source of truth (center vault / raw files).
4. Verify each piece before moving to the next.
5. If blocked, break the remaining work into smaller subsets and repeat.

Pitfall: Declaring impossibility is not an acceptable output.

## Evidence
> "If you come to that conclusion that what you need to do is start yourself over and you You need to do better pre-planning. You need to break it down into further sets and find a solution."
> "What you need to do when you come to a wall like that is go back and be more granular on previous steps."
