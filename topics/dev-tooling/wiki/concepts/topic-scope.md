# Topic Scope and Lens

This topic is the home for Chris's dev-tooling work: coding-assistant experiments, coder/review agents, sidecar, niwa, minimal Claude-Code alternatives, and decisions, preferences, pains, tools, people, and timelines tied to that work.

## Scope and anti-scope
In scope: anything about dev tooling or dev-tooling. Out of scope: general Hermes/fleet coordination -> `senter`, wiki UI -> `senter-wiki`, video/YouTube -> `youtube`, and anything that mentions another named topic more than this one. The cutter binds hard on `dev tooling` and `dev-tooling`.

## Lens
The watcher and agent read new context through these questions:
- What is Chris trying to achieve, in his own words?
- What changed since the last pass?
- Which statements are DECISIONS, which are PREFERENCES, PAINS, or open QUESTIONS?
- What did he say twice?

## Standing preferences
Root causes, never band-aids. Everything live and visible. Markdown + built-in Hermes over custom code. Bots first, then loops; reuse a fleet bot when one fits; skills auto-apply. No pitches about the build process itself.

## Suggestions shape
One line, phrased as a question Chris can say yes to, tied to a quote from him, naming the bot that would do it.
