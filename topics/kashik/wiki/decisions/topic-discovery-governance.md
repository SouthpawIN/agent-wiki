# Topic discovery governance

Chris decided to introduce a librarian concept that governs discovery across topics in the Senter wiki.

## What changed

Topic discovery becomes a governed capability rather than an emergent folder-and-filename convention. The librarian concept implies explicit rules for indexing, aliases, anti-scope enforcement, and cross-topic routing. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-07.md]

## Why

Chris wants the wiki process itself to be the product, with everything live and visible. A governed discovery layer keeps routing predictable and preserves his intent about how the vault should be used.

## Open questions

- Where does the librarian's authority come from: BRIEF files, a master registry, or both?
- How does the librarian signal a cut that should become its own topic instead of being absorbed?
