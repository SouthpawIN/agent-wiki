# Librarian concept

A governing discovery layer across all topics in the Senter wiki, owned conceptually by kashik.

## What Chris said

Chris introduced the librarian concept as a cross-topic governance idea. It implies one intelligence that owns discovery rules, topic linking, aliases, anti-scope boundaries, and routing decisions across the entire vault — not only within one topic wiki. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-07.md]

## Place in the fleet

This concept fits kashik's existing role as historian and librarian. It also points toward coordinating how topic boundaries are enforced fleet-wide without relying on ad hoc folder conventions.

## Open questions

- Should the librarian own alias resolution globally, or only per-topic aliases from BRIEF?
- Does the librarian maintain a master topic map, or derive routing from existing watcher cuts?
