Chris wants model research to go beyond a one-time lookup: a maintained, comparable set of **benchmarks** for each model in the widget, refreshed over time. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-16.md]

**What he wants:**
- "the best concrete benchmarks and what benchmarks we can find" for each model. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-16.md]
- Benchmarks for **more than just tokens per second** — he also wants performance benchmarked. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-16.md]
- The **most common benchmarks used to compare across all these models**, defined once so tracking is consistent. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-16.md]
- A way to **keep track of what can be found off the web for each model** as an "initial performance check." ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-16.md]

**Maintenance sub-agents.** Chris said "we should also have other kinds of sub agents for maintenance" — standing helpers that keep the benchmark data current rather than a single research pass. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-16.md]

**Widget behaviour (Hermes).** As tok/s and benchmarks are filled in per model, the widget auto-sorts to show which models are leading. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-16.md]
