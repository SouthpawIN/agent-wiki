## Ornith-1.5-35B-A3B (2026-09-15)
The "Ornith" handle resolves to **Ornith-1.5-35B-A3B** — a 35B all-MoE model with 3B active parameters. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-15.md]

A variant of it, **"Cyber-Tiel-Coder"**, was deployed and run through Hermes on Chris's machine: it reports "Hello. I'm Cyber-Tiel-Coder, a variant of Ornith-1.5-35B-A3B." ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-15.md]

**Performance on M1 32GB:** the full 22GB model at 65K context swaps and is too slow; it runs through Hermes only at a reduced context window. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-15.md]
