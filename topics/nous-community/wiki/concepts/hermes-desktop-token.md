## What it is
The Nous token is the authentication credential for a Hermes Desktop instance. It can become invalid and require a **reset** to restore access.

## Known pain
A token reset was observed on 2026-09-11 when Chris was helping someone else reset their Nous token on their own Hermes Desktop — a troubleshooting, not his own setup^[raw/inbox/2026-09-11.md].

## Scope note
Token auth is a general Hermes-desktop concern (belongs to `senter` per anti-scope), not a nous-community-specific concept. Recorded here only because the watcher filed the mention under `nous-community`. No preference or standing procedure about token management is stated anywhere yet.
