# Cognitive Distraction Trap

The bat-and-ball puzzle is a demonstration of a reasoning trap where an intuitive but wrong answer feels obviously correct. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-15.md]

## How it works
The solver subtracts the difference from the total (`1.10 − 1.00`) and stops at 10 cents, never checking that answer against the "a dollar more" constraint. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-15.md] The point of the puzzle, per Hermes, is precisely that *"people subtract 1.10 − 1.00 = 0.10 and stop there."* ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-15.md]

## Why it matters
It's a probe of whether a solver (human or model) verifies a result against all stated constraints instead of accepting the first number that fits the arithmetic. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-15.md]
