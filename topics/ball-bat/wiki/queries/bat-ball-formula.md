# Bat-and-Ball General Formula

The reusable solution method for the family of bat-and-ball puzzles, stated by Hermes when Chris's original left the difference blank. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-15.md]

## The formula
`ball = (total − difference) / 2` ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-15.md]

Derivation: let `x` = ball, bat = `x + difference`, so `x + (x + difference) = total` → `2x = total − difference` → `x = (total − difference) / 2`. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-15.md]

## Conditional use
Hermes flagged that the puzzle *"has a blank in it"* and offered: *"If the missing number wasn't $1.00, tell me and I'll redo it."* ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-15.md] The formula covers any total/difference without re-derivation.
