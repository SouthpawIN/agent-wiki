# HP laptop boot flash and Steam Play setup

During mid-September Chris was flashing and booting a new OS onto an **HP laptop** and running into bootloader and Steam setup questions along the way. This is a distinct device thread from the topic's core M1 model work, but it falls under the same device-embodiment framing — the HP is being driven by Hermes as one of its embodied machines. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-12.md]

## Boot / grub screen

At 20:29 on 2026-09-12 Chris reported the OS flash done and the machine sitting at the grub bootloader screen, asking what to do next:

> "Okay, the flash is complete. My HP is on the grub screen. What do I do now?" ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-12.md]

This is an in-progress setup step — no resolution was captured in the filing, only the question. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-12.md]

## Steam / Steam Play on the HP

The boot work connects to a broader goal of getting games running on the HP. A later compaction handoff snapshot on 2026-09-13 recorded an active task:

> "steam is running on my HP. I'm trying to get steam play to [find it's having a hard time]" ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-13.md]

So the picture is: HP running a fresh OS, then **Steam** installed and Chris working to get **Steam Play (Proton)** functioning so games will launch. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-13.md] Steam Play is the Proton compatibility layer that lets Windows games run on Linux, which explains the "having a hard time" — a Proton/compatibility troubleshooting thread. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-13.md]
