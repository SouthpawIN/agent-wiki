# Rename context to wiki

Chris decided to rename everything that is currently context into wiki. He believes the word "context" is confusing some of the building process, and he wants the topic's context section to become a full wiki for that topic. The intent is that each topic gets its own wiki, skills, bots, and loops, with the wiki becoming the main surface for that topic's knowledge and steerability.

## What

Rename the topic-level context section to wiki so that topic context is read as a topic wiki.

## Why

The word context is confusing builders; a wiki framing makes the topic a first-class knowledge surface.

2026-09-07
