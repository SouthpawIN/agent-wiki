# Chromium New Tab as Center Wiki

Chris wants to modify Chromium so that the center wiki is its home page / new-tab page — so that opening a new tab surfaces the center wiki directly, instead of a blank tab.

> How about this? Can we modify the Chromium on here so that this center wiki is the home page for it and then we should. I want to be able to click a new tab like right now there's one tab open for center wiki. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-12.md]

He distinguished this from talking about the center wiki itself — he is really talking about "the Hermes Desktop around it" (2026-09-11), i.e. the browser/desktop chrome surrounding the wiki rather than the wiki content.
