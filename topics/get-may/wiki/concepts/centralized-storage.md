Chris plans to use the **Omarchy's 16 TB** as a **central storage hub** to "free up my phone and my Google drives and my other devices as well."^[raw/inbox/2026-09-15.md] Once all devices are connected over **Tailscale**, he wants to do "a big reorganization thing" — consolidating media and files onto the Omarchy and off phone, Google Drive, and other machines.^[raw/inbox/2026-09-15.md]

The MacBook is the control point for this: it runs **OBS and media edits**, and the Omarchy supplies the storage.^[raw/inbox/2026-09-15.md]

Outstanding: define what gets migrated, where it lives on the 16 TB, and how devices mount and access the shared store over Tailscale.
