**OBS** (OBS Studio) is the streaming software Chris wants **installed on his MacBook**.^[raw/inbox/2026-09-14.md] He wants the MacBook — not the Omarchy — to run OBS so it can capture and manage the stream while he plays on the Omarchy.^[raw/inbox/2026-09-14.md] As of 2026-09-15 Hermes was proceeding to install it.^[raw/inbox/2026-09-15.md]

OBS on the MacBook is part of a broader plan for the MacBook to **handle OBS and media edits**, and, once everything is connected, to serve as the media/storage hub drawing on the Omarchy's 16 TB.^[raw/inbox/2026-09-15.md]
