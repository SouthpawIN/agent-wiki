**Omarchy** is Chris's custom arch-based Linux device (dictated variously as "Omarchy", "Omar"/"Omar key", and "obarcus").^[raw/inbox/2026-09-13.md]^[raw/inbox/2026-09-15.md] It is becoming the central machine in his setup: it holds **16 TB** of storage and runs a desktop OS over which Chris plays games such as **Armored Core 6**.^[raw/inbox/2026-09-14.md]^[raw/inbox/2026-09-15.md]

It is reached over **Tailscale** at a tail IP, and Chris says it shares the **same sudo password as the MacBook** — which he has been struggling to recall and recover.^[raw/inbox/2026-09-15.md] As of 2026-09-15 his **hermes install on Omarchy is broken**: neither the `hermes` CLI nor the desktop works, and he wants the working steps documented for next time.^[raw/inbox/2026-09-15.md] A likely root cause surfaced 2026-09-13: his "Turmix" (likely T Remix) OS environment "was messed up," so he reinstalled Turmix and was reinstalling Hermes.^[raw/inbox/2026-09-13.md]
