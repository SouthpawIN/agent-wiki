# Wiki page quality bar

## The bar, as demonstrated
The 18:15 spot-check of one compiled decision page is the only worked example so far of what a compiled page must do. Read as a checklist, a page passes when it has: ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-05.md]

1. **Chris's words verbatim**, with `^[raw/…]` provenance pointing at the raw inbox line they came from.
2. **The *why* traced to pains he actually voiced** — "traced to two pains you voiced that afternoon", not a paraphrased rationale.
3. **What it supersedes** — the earlier decision or rule it replaces, named.
4. **Conflicts kept, not resolved** — the earlier "wait for enough skills" flywheel vs "shouldn't have to wait" was flagged and marked `contested`, with dates, per [[topics/senter-wiki/wiki/concepts/llm-wiki-topic-layout|the LLM-wiki layout]] rule "contradictions kept with dates".
5. **An open question the author missed** — "where merge/split governance lives now that the umbrella collapsed into the topic agent". A page that only restates is below the bar; it should surface the next question.

## Where the bar comes from
`SCHEMA.md` conventions (frontmatter, ≥2 wikilinks, provenance, `<!-- chris -->` lines never rewritten) are the *floor*; the 18:15 check is the *ceiling* Chris was shown and accepted without correction. It is consistent with his standing rule that a claim without evidence is a lie to him — see [[topics/senter-wiki/wiki/concepts/chris-ui-working-rules|Chris's working rules]]. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-05-legacy-inbox.md]

## Known ways pages fall short (observed in this topic on 2026-09-05)
- Two compiles writing the same subject under different slugs → near-duplicates that both pass the floor but split the evidence ([[topics/senter-wiki/wiki/queries/duplicate-page-candidates|candidates]]).
- Hermes narration quoted as if it were Chris's view — the inbox is mostly `hermes` speaker lines; only `chris` lines are his words. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-05-legacy-inbox.md]
