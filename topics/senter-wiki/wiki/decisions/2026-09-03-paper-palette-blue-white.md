# `--paper` = cool blue-white; girl = masked paper (2026-09-03)

**What.** Every "white" resolves to `--paper` (`#dfe8f5` at 96 %); the wallpaper picture is a luminance mask over flat paper, stacked twice; grey never used as a colour;
exemptions `#FFE45A` and the black EQ dots. ^[raw/sources/knowledge/ref-vault-live-palette.md]

**Why.** Chris compares UI text and the girl's ink side by side and reads mismatch as *"cream" / "warm" / "sad"*. Three tint approaches failed
([[topics/senter-wiki/wiki/comparisons/wallpaper-tint-approaches|comparison]]); `brightness` filters dimmed the stars and broke `position:fixed`. He wanted *"blue-ish off-white"* — cool
`#e9ecef` still read as cream; *"when he says cool, go visibly blue."* **Proof standard.** Pixel triples, not vision. See
[[topics/senter-wiki/wiki/entities/wallpaper-girl|paper palette]].
