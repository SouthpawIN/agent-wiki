# Discoverable tail-scale model

A model Chris is looking for after restarting — one that is **discoverable** and **tail-scale**.
He states it as a need, not yet a plan: "I need that" [[topics/restarted-seeing/wiki/concepts/discoverable-tail-scale-model|context]].

## What Chris said (verbatim)
- 2026-09-15 18:21: "I just restarted and I'm not seeing the discoverable tail scale model. I need that."  ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-15.md]

## Interpretation
Two adjectives matter:
- **Discoverable** — the model (or the thing that finds/uses it) should surface itself, not sit hidden. This maps to Senter's
  discoverability theme (a discoverable tail-scale model would be one the fleet or wiki can find on its own).
- **Tail-scale** — long-tail, low-frequency, small-probability cases at scale (cf. "tail-scale" in the long-context /
  rare-event sense used across his topics).

Both words together suggest a model that handles the long tail of rare cases and is reachable/discoverable by the system
rather than hand-triggered. Nothing about it has been built or decided yet — this is the seed of an idea.
