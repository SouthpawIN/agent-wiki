# Proactive Repair Over Workarounds

Chris drew a sharp line between "finding workarounds" and fixing the actual damage. When a project state is broken, the required response is ownership, repair, and a root-cause path — not an excuse or bypass.

## Standard

> "You need to be proactive instead of finding workarounds. You have screwed up my project. You need to take responsibility for that and work to rectify that instead of being lazy." ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-06.md]

## Rejected failure mode

> "And telling me that you can't reconstruct it is not an answer." ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-06.md]

## Implications

- Repair must lead with a concrete recovery plan, not a declaration of impossibility.
- Responsibility is non-negotiable even when backups or snapshots are incomplete.
- Granular backtracking is the accepted technique, not one-shot regeneration.
