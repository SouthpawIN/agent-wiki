# Pre-Planning and Recovery

Chris explicitly required a methodology when a rebuild is blocked: break work into smaller steps and rebuild from the source rather than attempting an all-at-once recovery.

## Directives

> "If you come to that conclusion that what you need to do is start yourself over and you You need to do better pre-planning. You need to break it down into further sets and find a solution. Not a solution is not a solution." ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-06.md]

> "What you need to do when you come to a wall like that is go back and be more granular on previous steps." ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-06.md]
