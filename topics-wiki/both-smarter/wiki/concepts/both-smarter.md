Chris wants the model to be **both smarter and faster** than free models, not merely one or the other. This is a standing preference for his model selection and tooling. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-07.md]

He wants a concrete gauge of intelligence backed by published benchmarks rather than vibes or in-session performance. When asking for model research, he explicitly does not want regurgitation of information already known in the conversation.
