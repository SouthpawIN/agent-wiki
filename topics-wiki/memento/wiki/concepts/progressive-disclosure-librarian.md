# Progressive-disclosure librarian

Chris proposed using progressive disclosure for the whole Senter surface: discover skills first through frontmatter, then dig deeper, while a librarian watches the entire vault and progressively surfaces wiki content, skills, bots, and loops. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-07-from-senter.md]

## Quote

> We can use progressive disclosure just like how skills are discovered by first through the font matter and then it could dig deeper. We can have a librarian watching the whole thing and progressively discovering, wiki skills, bots, and loops.
> — Chris, 2026-09-07 18:15 ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-07-from-senter.md]

## Summary

The idea is to make the vault itself the discovery interface, starting from the compact metadata already present in skill frontmatter and expanding into deeper wiki pages, bot capabilities, and loop schedules as the user explores. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-07-from-senter.md]
