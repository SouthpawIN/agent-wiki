# Bonsai 27B

Bonsai is one of the three final local inference candidates Chris selected for the M1 32GB benchmark sweep. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-07.md]

## Identified forms

- Ternary Bonsai 27B MLX 2-bit, ~7.91 GB. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-07.md]
- 1.58-bit variant: seen as a promising next step because it is expected to run faster on M1. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-07.md]
- Quote: "Ternary Bonsai 27B MLX 2-bit: 7.91 GB! At 1.58-bit, this will absolutely fly on M1." ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-07.md]

## Status

- Found and downloaded by 2026-09-07. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-07.md]
- Not yet benchmarked under the finalized warmup → short → 5K protocol at the time of the latest raw item. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-07.md]
- Treated as a primary candidate for TurboFit auxiliary inference on this machine. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-07.md]
