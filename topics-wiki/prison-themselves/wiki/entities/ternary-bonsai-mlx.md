# Ternary Bonsai MLX

A 2-bit ternary variant of Bonsai MLX that Chris added to his running set on 2026-09-07. His rationale was speed — "it's 2-bit ternary, might be faster."^[raw/inbox/2026-09-07.md]

## Rationale: speed
Chris's interest in the ternary variant was explicitly performance-driven: he added it because the 2-bit ternary quantization "might be faster." This fits his broader pattern of chasing faster local inference across the model family. [[topics-wiki/prison-themselves/wiki/entities/bonsai-mlx|Bonsai MLX]] is the base model it derives from.
