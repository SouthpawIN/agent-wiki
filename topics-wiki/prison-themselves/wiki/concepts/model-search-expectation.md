# Model Search Expectation

Chris expects deep, thorough searching of model sources (e.g. HuggingFace) before a conclusion is drawn — and calls it out sharply when it isn't done. This is a recurring standard he holds for any model-research task. [[topics-wiki/prison-themselves/wiki/concepts/local-mlx-model-fleet|Local MLX Model Fleet]] is the domain this expectation applies to.

## The 09-07 rebuke
On 2026-09-07 12:02 Chris said: "Prison ML themselves the people who make bonsai have an MO have an ML X variant You did not search to the level that I expect you to."^[raw/inbox/2026-09-07.md] He was pushing for a specific MLX variant of the bonsai model and dissatisfied with the depth of the prior search. Hermes responded by searching HuggingFace properly for the "Prism ML MLX variant."

## What "the level I expect" means
The phrasing ("an MO," "an ML X variant") suggests Chris expects the agent to surface model lineages, variants, and quantization options rather than surface hits. The bar is thoroughness about the model family, not just a single lookup.
