# OBS streaming from the Omarchy for Armored Core 6

On 2026-09-14 Chris described wanting to manage a gaming stream from the MacBook:

> "I also want OBS on this MacBook. I am playing Armored Core 6 on my Omarchy and I would like to manage streaming from my MacBook instead of t[ugging...]" ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-14.md]

## The setup

The intent is to install **OBS** on the MacBook and use it as the capture/streaming manager while Chris plays **Armored Core 6** on his **Omarchy** (a Steam-Deck-style handheld). ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-14.md] The goal is convenience — managing the stream from the MacBook rather than having to reach over to the handheld. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-14.md]

## Status: pending

As of the last filing the task is still open. The watcher captured that a follow-up "try again" was sent after a **443 probe** was explicitly stopped, and the OBS/Omarchy request remains:

> "...still active, pending. Follow-up input was 'try again' after the 443 probe was explicitly stopped." ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-14.md]

So the concrete open questions are: how to capture the Omarchy's output into OBS on the MacBook (USB capture vs. network stream), and how to keep that stream manageable from the MacBook's side. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-14.md]
