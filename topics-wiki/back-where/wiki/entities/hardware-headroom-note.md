# Hardware headroom note for M1 32GB

## Note
Chris treated the 32GB M1 MacBook as available headroom rather than a hard ceiling during recovery. He said the machine should have enough space to run an audition step without blocking background testing, expecting parallel or trial workloads to coexist.

> "You have 32 gigabytes, um, and the other one is testing in the background. You should have enough space to at least do a step and not get in the way." ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-07.md]
