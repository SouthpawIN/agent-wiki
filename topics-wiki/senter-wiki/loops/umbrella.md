# umbrella
owner: senter · cadence: every 30m · status: LIVE · runner: launchd `com.senter.umbrella` every 30m (`assets/umbrella.py`)
Opinionated pass over all topics: similarity-based merge/split proposals, cross-topic skill dedupe, promote skill clusters to
bots, refresh `topics-wiki/_global` catalogs and every topic's `INDEX.md`. Everything it wants to change is a card; nothing applies
without Chris — but it should *propose boldly* (Chris: "opinionated in what it combines").
