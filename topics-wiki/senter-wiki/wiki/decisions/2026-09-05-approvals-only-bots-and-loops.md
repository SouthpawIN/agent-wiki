# Approvals only for bots and loops (2026-09-05 17:19–17:25)

**What.** *"Policy changed as you asked — no approvals for topics, edits, continuity, or skills — they auto-apply … Only bots and loops remain `.proposed` with APPROVE/REJECT."*
Also: a new subject triggers immediate scaffold + research dispatch to an existing bot (looker) — *"No card, no waiting."* ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-05-legacy-inbox.md]

**Why.** Bots and loops *"cost money and run unattended"*; skills and wiki edits are cheap and reversible, and gating them stalled the feed. **Evidence of effect.** 5 pending
skills promoted at once; topic-worker writes `SKILL.md` directly. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-05-legacy-inbox.md] ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-05.md]

Supersedes the July spec's all-proposal MVP ([[topics-wiki/senter-wiki/wiki/concepts/approval-policy|policy page]]). Related: [[topics-wiki/senter-wiki/wiki/entities/umbrella|umbrella]].
