## Interruptions
Dictation sometimes cuts a sentence ("make them…"): brief the clear part, leave an explicit open slot for the rest. "I didn't clap you. Please continue with what you were doing. I didn't mean to stop you" — an interrupt is not a stop; resume. Related: [[topics-wiki/senter/wiki/concepts/fleet-coordination-lessons|lessons]].
