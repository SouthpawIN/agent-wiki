# librarian-progressive-discovery
1. Read the topic INDEX before proposing anything. 2. Order discovery: frontmatter, file, section, artifact. 3. Propose only one next artifact at a time. 4. Prefer existing pages over new ones; link instead of duplicating. 5. Route changes: wiki edits auto-apply; bots-profiles/loops go to sidebar cards only. 6. Log every proposal to the global index. Pitfalls: do not load an entire topic; do not propose many cards at once; do not act on unresolved cards until approved.

## Evidence
> We can have a librarian watching the whole thing and progressively discovering, wiki skills, bots, and loops.
> We can use progressive disclosure just like how skills are discovered by first through the font matter and then it could dig deeper.
