# Every message, one profile, one card (2026-09-05 16:35)

**What.** Three open questions settled by Chris in one line — *"Per message, live, visible"*:
1. The watcher files **every message** (not batches) — *"per message, like Cursor."*
2. **One senter profile runs the loops**; per-topic profiles come later, *"when a topic earns a bot."*
3. **One card per proposal** so the feed moves constantly. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-05-legacy-inbox.md] ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-05.md]

**Why.** Motion and visibility: Chris judges the system by what he sees change in the wiki as he talks. **Consequence.** The watcher was built first (*"the piece that makes
everything move"*); topic-worker → umbrella → approvals followed the same afternoon. See [[topics-wiki/senter-wiki/wiki/entities/senter-watcher|watcher]],
[[topics-wiki/senter-wiki/wiki/concepts/ui-performs-the-fleet|UI performs the fleet]].
