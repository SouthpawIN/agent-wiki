# Steam Link (app)

**Steam Link** is the app Chris uses to stream games from his steam machine (the HP/Omarchy) to another screen. It is free (App Store "Get").^[raw/inbox/2026-09-13.md]

- Installed on the **MacBook** to stream from the HP.^[raw/inbox/2026-09-13.md]
