## The requirement
On 2026-09-12 Chris asked for "the central area that we are looking at where the central wiki is. Next to there should be a plus sign to open a new tab on a browser"^[raw/inbox/2026-09-12.md]. He was frustrated it wasn't there: "Jesus Christ you are a dog"^[raw/inbox/2026-09-12.md].

## Scope
He clarified on 2026-09-11 that this is "specifically for working within Hermes Desktop with the new tab and the sessions and bot options that we usually see on the default setup. I would like you to figure that out. I'm not talking about the center wiki but I am talking about the Hermes Desktop around it"^[raw/inbox/2026-09-11.md].

So this topic owns the **Hermes Desktop chrome** (tabs, sessions, bot options), not the center-wiki content itself.

## Status
The `+` / new-tab button must stay visible even after closing tabs — on 2026-09-14 Chris reported "in the plus sign to add another tab is gone I closed out a couple tabs, but I still want to see the plus sign to add more maybe got blocked somewhere"^[raw/inbox/2026-09-14.md]. See [[plus-sign-disappears|plus-sign query]] and [[webview-vs-preview-pane|webview vs preview pane]].
