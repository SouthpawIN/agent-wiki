# MacBook Case Status & Compatibility Decision

Chris asked for a status on "those" (downloads) and a verdict on whether the matte black case he found is right for his MacBook. This page records the answer he was given.^[raw/inbox/2026-09-16.md]

## What was decided
- **The case fits.** A matte black case for "MacBook Pro 14 inch" will fit Chris's MacBook Pro 14-inch (M1 Pro, 2021, MacBookPro18,3).
- **Why:** the M1 Pro 14-inch chassis is identical to the M2 Pro/Max (2023) and M3 Pro/Max (2024) 14-inch models — cases are cross-compatible across 2021–2024.

## The preference / rule Chris was handed
- **Only buy if the listing explicitly says "MacBook Pro 14-inch 2021-2024" (or an equivalent 2021–2024 span).** A bare "14-inch" label is not a fit guarantee. This is Chris's guardrail against buying the wrong case.
