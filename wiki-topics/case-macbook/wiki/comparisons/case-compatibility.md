# 14-inch MacBook Pro Case Compatibility

The whole point of Chris's shopping question — does a "MacBook Pro 14-inch" case fit *his* machine, and what to look for?^[raw/inbox/2026-09-16.md]

## The compatibility rule
The 14-inch MacBook Pro chassis has been **physically identical across the M1 Pro, M2 Pro/Max, and M3 Pro/Max generations (2021–2024)**. Therefore:
- A case built for "MacBook Pro 14-inch 2021–2024" fits all of them, including Chris's M1 Pro 2021 (MacBookPro18,3).
- Cross-generation fit is a given — screw positions, port cutouts, and body dimensions did not change between these chips.

## The shopping guardrail
- **Buy only if the listing explicitly states "MacBook Pro 14-inch 2021-2024" (or an equivalent 2021–2024 span).**
- A bare "14-inch" label is **not** a fit guarantee — Chris was told to demand the year range before purchasing.

## Why this matters to Chris
He's about to buy a matte black case on Amazon and doesn't want to waste money on one that doesn't fit. The guardrail is the cheap insurance that the "14-inch" case he found is actually the right one.
