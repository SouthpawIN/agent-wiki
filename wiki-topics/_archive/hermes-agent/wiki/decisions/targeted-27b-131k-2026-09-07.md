# Targeted 27B at 131K — 2026-09-07

On `2026-09-07`, Chris explicitly required the active provider to be the `Qwen 3.8 27B` variant, with a target of `10+ tok/s` at `131K` context.^[raw/inbox/2026-09-07.md] This requirement contests the earlier stable-context recommendation of `65K` context and the Hermes-side lock of `Unleashed + DFlash2` at `131K`.

## What
- Required model variant: `Qwen 3.8 27B`.
- Required throughput target: `10+ tok/s` at `131K` context.
- Evaluation context: active Hermes session/stack, after environment cleanup.

## Why
- Chris wants the `27B` model tested at the long-context target rather than accepting a reduced-context fallback.
- This is a performance target, not merely a stability preference.
- **Physical reality check:** `Qwen 3.8 27B Q3_K_XL = 12.3GB` + `131K KV cache ~8GB` + `DFlash2 1GB` + `macOS+Hermes 6-8GB` = **27.3-29.3GB** against `32GB` physical memory — OOM inevitable.^[raw/inbox/2026-09-07.md]

## Open question
Whether `M1 32GB` can deliver `10+ tok/s` at `131K` with `Qwen 3.8 27B`, or whether the requirement implies a hardware/config path change.

**Answer from evidence (2026-09-07 05:42):** **No.** The target is physically impossible on current hardware. The `10+ tok/s at 131K` requirement can only be met with `64GB+` Mac upgrade or faster memory bus (M5 Max reference: `58 tok/s`).^[raw/inbox/2026-09-07.md]
