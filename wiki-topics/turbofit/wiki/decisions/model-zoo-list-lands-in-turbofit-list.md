## Model Zoo Models for the TurboFit List

## The request

> "I gave you a list of a bunch of ones to include from the Model Zoo to be on the Turbo Fit list. Please, please do better."

^[provenance: click-options/raw/inbox/2026-09-16.md]

## What he wants
Every model from his Model Zoo list present on the TurboFit provider — not a partial subset. This is the same goal as TurboFit Models Grouped Under the TurboFit Provider (2026-09-15): all his models, grouped, discoverable, with no gaps.

## Context / pain
This lands on top of the ongoing TurboFit-provider problem: the provider has repeatedly **failed to appear** in the Hermes desktop picker (see TurboFit Provider Still Missing from the Desktop Picker (2026-09-15)) and the acceptance gate is testing **through Hermes** as a user, not the raw endpoint (Test Through Hermes, Not Straight to the Endpoint). The "please do better" is frustration that the model list he gave did not fully land.
