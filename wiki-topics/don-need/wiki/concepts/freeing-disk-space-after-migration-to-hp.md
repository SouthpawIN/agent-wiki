# Freeing disk space after migrating games to the HP

Chris said the games downloaded on the current computer "are all on the HP" now and "don't run super well on this computer either," so they "don't need" them here anymore — "several gigabytes" that could be freed.^[raw/inbox/2026-09-13.md]

The games already live on the HP, so the local copies are pure dead weight; what remains is the cleanup step of uninstalling them on the current machine. This is a one-time teardown, not a recurring job.^[raw/inbox/2026-09-13.md]
