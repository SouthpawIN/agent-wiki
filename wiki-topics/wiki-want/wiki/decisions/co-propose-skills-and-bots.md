# Co-propose skills and bots

Chris wants skills and bots to be proposed simultaneously whenever both are needed for a topic. This keeps the topic's build surface coherent and prevents proposal drift.

## What

Propose skills and bots together when a topic needs both, instead of staging them separately.

## Why

Separate staging can split the build surface; co-proposal keeps the topic's intelligence package coherent.
