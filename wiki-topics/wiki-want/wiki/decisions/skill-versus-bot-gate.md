# Skill-versus-bot gate

Chris wants a clear gate between skills and bots. A skill is something that can be added to existing intelligence, an existing bot, or to Hermes itself. A bot is something that needs persistence and intelligence of its own. This gate should be stated explicitly so topic proposals know whether to card a skill, a bot, or both at the same time.

## What

Create an explicit skill-versus-bot gate for topic proposals: skill = added ability; bot = persistence and intelligence needed.

## Why

Without a clear gate, topics proposal drift. Co-proposing skills and bots makes the topic build coherent and keeps infrastructure choices in one place.

## When

2026-09-07

## Evidence

- "I want there to be a queer definition on the difference between what would make a good skill and what would make a good bot, you know, whether it's something that needs persistence and intelligence or if it's an ability that can be more efficiently added to existing intelligence, existing bot or to Hermes itself." ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-07.md]
