Chris wants the model table/widget to be a **portal**: instead of a blurb sitting as an item on the table, each model should be *clickable* and open its *own page* containing information about that model — specifically research done on its Hugging Face page. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-16.md]

> "Instead of the blurb being an item on the table, I want to be able to click on each model and it goes to its own page, where there's information about that model, and by blurb I wanted research to be done on a hugging face." ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-16.md]

**Portal as a template.** Chris frames this as "kind of a portal type for how I want other topics to go within this wiki" — i.e. a reusable presentation pattern (clickable cards → dedicated research pages) he wants applied to other topics, not just the model list. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-16.md] He noted the agent need not do all the research for every entry at once. ^[raw/inbox/2026-09-16.md]
