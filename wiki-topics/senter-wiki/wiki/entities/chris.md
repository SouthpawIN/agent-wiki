# Chris (sovthpaw) — the user

## Who
Chris (GitHub `SouthpawIN`, machine user `sovthpaw`) owns the Senter vault and drives the Senter Wiki UI live by **voice dictation** in the Hermes desktop preview pane. He works with the Hermes Agent team and wants the process itself to be the product — "exactly how I intend people to use this." ^[raw/sources/knowledge/skill-agent-wiki-vault.md] ^[raw/sources/knowledge/ref-vault-live-demo-video.md]

## His vision, in his words
"This is a Cursor x Obsidian setup where everything you talk about gets captured as Topics in a wiki … Jump between topics and it sorts and automates them all, looking for efficiencies and reusable artifacts." Vocabulary he fixed: **Agent** = the harness (Hermes), **Bot** = a profile with SOUL.md, **Sub-agents** = drones. See [[wiki-topics/senter-wiki/wiki/concepts/senter-wiki-vision|the vision]] and [[wiki-topics/senter-wiki/wiki/concepts/goop-four-primitives|four primitives]]. ^[raw/sources/knowledge/ref-vault-live-concept-2026-09-03.md]

## How he works (compiled)
- Tunes UI by micro-increments ("shift it about 10 pixels" → "and about 30 more") and locks exact numbers; every tweak must be reloaded in his preview and visually verified. ^[raw/sources/knowledge/ref-vault-live-settings-card-2026-09-03.md]
- Reverses just-made requests after seeing them — normal iteration; restated navigation THREE times on 2026-09-03 and expects the newest statement to be the whole spec. ^[raw/sources/knowledge/skill-agent-wiki-vault.md]
- Reads colour as signal (`#FFE45A` = attention only), compares whites side by side, distrusts vision verdicts and accepts pixel triples. ^[raw/sources/knowledge/ref-vault-live-palette.md]
- Aligns CENTRES, not edges; means the RIGHT edge when he says "line up". ^[raw/sources/knowledge/skill-agent-wiki-vault.md]
- Voice cuts off mid-sentence; stray TV/kid audio arrives as garbled messages. ^[raw/sources/knowledge/skill-agent-wiki-vault.md]
- Wants the UI *driven*, not simulated: "practice your computer use, not work around." ^[raw/sources/knowledge/ref-vault-live-ui-2026-09-03.md]
- Invents workflow fixes himself: hand-placement mode ("make them click-and-draggable and resizable, I'll place them, then say go"). ^[raw/sources/knowledge/ref-vault-live-hud-place-2026-09-03.md]
Full rule set: [[wiki-topics/senter-wiki/wiki/concepts/chris-ui-working-rules|Chris's UI working rules]].
